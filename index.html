OUR STORY

It started with a curious child.

The story of ParenBond begins with Kaustubh Kumbhar, when he was in 6th standard. Expressive, playful, a little mischievous and endlessly innovative, Kaustubh had a natural way of looking at everyday situations differently.

He was inspired by his father, Dr. Vidyadhar Kumbhar, the concept inventor of the Brain & Brawn cartoon world. Based on the principles of Ayurveda, the characters bring familiar ideas to life: Vatu represents Vata, Pittu represents Pitta, Kappu represents Kapha, and Mana represents the brain and mind—the part that guides thoughts, actions and development.

Together, this sparked a simple idea: what if children could learn important life lessons through small stories and activities, instead of simply being told what to do?

When a child understands the reason behind a healthy habit, a value or a choice, behaving well doesn't have to feel like a burden. And when children understand the why, parents don't have to keep repeating only the what.

Tell less.Explain the why.Understand more.
